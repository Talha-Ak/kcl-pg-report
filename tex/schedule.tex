\chapter{Project Schedule}
\label{ch:schedule}

The project is scheduled to be completed by the given deadline, 7th August 2025.
A timeline outlining the specific tasks and their durations
is illustrated in the Gantt chart available in Figure~\ref{fig:gantt_chart}.

\begin{figure}[h]
    \centering
    \includestandalone[width=0.5\textwidth]{figures/gantt-chart}
    \caption{Gantt chart illustrating the project schedule.}
    \label{fig:gantt_chart}
\end{figure}

The schedule allocates time across several key phases, beginning with initial setup,
final literature checks, and verification of the prepared datasets (Weeks 1--2).
The core experimental phase, involving running the Conformal Risk Control evaluations
on the CheXpert and NIH Chest X-ray datasets using the selected models, is planned for Weeks 3--6.
Subsequent analysis of the experimental results and comparison
between different approaches will occur during Weeks 6--8.
The final weeks (Weeks 9--12) are dedicated to preparing the required 10-minute presentation
and completing the writing and finalization of this report,
allowing for overlap between these tasks.
